BILLBANAO / HELP & SUPPORT

A little help, whenever you need it.

Your guide to a simpler billing day.

Get started in three steps

  1. Open My business, enter your business name and contact details, and save. A logo is optional.
  2. Select New invoice or New quotation. Add a client, items, dates, currency, and any discount or tax.
  3. Save the document. Download the PDF, print it or share it using your device's share menu.

Where are my records?

They are saved on this device, in this browser profile or Android WebView. Another browser, device or website address will have a separate set of records. There is no cloud account or automatic cross-device sync.

How do I move to another device?

Choose My business → Export backup. Keep the JSON file safe. On your other device, open BillBanao and choose Restore backup. Restoring replaces that device's current records; it does not merge them. Export both devices first if you need to preserve both sets.

Can I use it without internet?

On a supported HTTPS browser, open the app online once and let its files finish loading. The application files are cached for later offline use. Creating documents, managing records and generating PDFs then work locally. Clearing the site's storage also removes offline files. The first visit, updates and opening external links need connectivity.

How do payments and quotations work?

Payment status is manual. Viewing or downloading a bill does not mark it as paid. Open a saved invoice and select Mark as paid when payment is actually received. A quotation can be converted into a new, separately numbered invoice; the original quotation is kept.

How is tax calculated?

Each item total is rounded to two decimal places. Discount is calculated on the subtotal; tax is calculated after discount. Tax and discount support 0–100% with up to two decimal places. Check the rate and calculation against your business requirements.

PDF and printing

Download PDF creates a file locally, including Urdu text. PDFs use rendered page images, so their text is not selectable. For selectable text where supported, use Print and your browser's Save as PDF option. A native Android WebView needs download, sharing and printing support from its host app; a bare WebView wrapper may not provide them.

Storage and recovery

Export backups regularly, especially after adding many records. If storage is full or blocked, the app reports that saving failed. Keep the form open, then free space or export a backup. If a saved record becomes unreadable, the recovery screen lets you download the original data before resetting. A recovery file is for diagnosis and is not necessarily a valid restore backup.

Make a correction

Use Edit from Documents to change a saved document. Business profile changes apply only to new documents: old ones retain their saved business details and logo. Deleting a client does not change old invoices.

Need more help?

Contact the publisher. Include the app version and a description of the issue. Avoid including private client details.
