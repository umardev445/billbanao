BILLBANAO / YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Your business records belong to you. This page explains what the default BillBanao app processes, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

Effective date: 7 November 2026
App: BillBanao
Publisher: The distributor of BillBanao

Preview notice: The publisher has not yet provided verified contact details for this build. These must be completed before public distribution.

1. Information you enter

BillBanao processes the business name, logo, contact details and address you provide; client names and contact details; invoice and quotation items, amounts, dates, notes and status; and your language and appearance preferences. You choose what to enter. Please enter client information only when you are authorised to use it.

2. Local storage and purpose

The default app saves these records in your browser or Android WebView's local storage on your device. It uses them to prepare documents, calculate totals, organise records and generate PDFs and backups. This code contains no account system, cloud synchronisation, analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels or remote AI processing. Invoice and client records are not sent to a BillBanao server by this code.

Local storage is not an encrypted vault. People with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access it. Your browser or operating system may perform its own backup or synchronisation under its separate settings and policies.

3. Website hosting and network requests

When you open the hosted website or check for app updates, the website host receives normal web-request information, which can include an IP address, request time, browser information and requested file path. If this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub handles that information under its privacy statement. Other distributors may use another host. This app does not put invoice or client records in request URLs or upload them to that host.

4. PDFs, images, backups and sharing

A logo you select is resized and processed locally. PDFs are generated on your device. A backup contains your business details, client information, documents and preferences in an unencrypted JSON file. Keep backups in a safe place and share them only with people you trust.

Download, print and share actions happen only when you choose them. If you share a PDF using your device's share menu, the selected app or recipient will receive the file and handle it under its own policies. There is no automatic upload to WhatsApp, email or a cloud drive.

5. Permissions, cookies and tracking

The web app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone or SMS access. A file picker lets you choose an individual logo or backup file. Downloading or sharing may use your browser or operating system's normal controls. The app uses local storage for records and preferences, and a service-worker cache for application files. It does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

6. Retention and deletion

Records remain in this browser profile or WebView until you delete them, clear the site's storage, remove the app's data, or the browser or operating system removes them. Use My business → Delete all app data to remove BillBanao's saved business details, clients, invoices and preferences from this installation. Individual clients and documents can also be deleted. Deleting a client does not erase their details from previously saved invoices; delete those documents separately if needed.

Deleting app data does not remove already exported PDFs, backup files, copies sent to recipients, hosting logs, or backups maintained by your operating system. Remove those copies separately where applicable. There is no BillBanao user account to delete. See data deletion instructions.

7. Support messages

If you contact the publisher, your email provider and the publisher receive the message, sender address and information you choose to include. That information is used to answer your request. Do not send client records or private backup files unless necessary for your request. You can request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Children

BillBanao is a general business utility and is not designed or marketed for children. Its default code does not create online user accounts or collect child profiles.

9. Changes and contact

The publisher must keep this policy consistent with the version distributed, including any hosting, native Android features or third-party SDKs added later. The effective date above identifies the current policy. For privacy questions, contact the BillBanao publisher at the verified address on the Contact page, once configured.
