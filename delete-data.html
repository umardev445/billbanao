BILLBANAO / DATA DELETION

Your data. Your decision.

BillBanao has no user account or cloud record database in this build.

Delete everything saved by the app

  1. Open BillBanao on the device and browser profile where you used it.
  2. Open My business.
  3. Optionally select Export backup if you want to retain a copy.
  4. Select Delete all app data and confirm.

This removes the app's saved business profile and logo, clients, invoices, quotations and preferences from this installation. Static application files may remain cached for offline use. To remove those too, clear the site's browser storage or remove the Android app's storage through your device settings.

Delete individual records

Use the delete action on Documents or Clients. Deleting a client leaves their details on existing invoices and quotations. Delete those documents separately if needed.

Copies outside the app

Remove downloaded PDFs and backup JSON files from Downloads, cloud storage or other folders yourself. Recipients retain copies you sent them. Browser, device backups and website hosting logs are managed under the relevant provider's separate controls and policies.

Support correspondence

For a request concerning information you sent directly to the publisher, use the Contact page. Do not send an entire invoice backup merely to request deletion.
