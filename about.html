BILLBANAO / ABOUT BILLBANAO

Small business. Big possibilities.

A calm, simple place for your everyday billing.

BillBanao is an offline-first invoice and quotation maker for freelancers, independent professionals and small businesses. Create documents, save client details, track manual payment status and take your records with you in a backup file.

Made to stay simple

  • English and Urdu interface, including right-to-left layouts.
  • Six currencies: PKR, INR, USD, AED, GBP and EUR.
  • Invoices and quotations with automatic totals, discounts and tax.
  • Local PDF generation, printing and supported device sharing.
  • Client records, local backup and restore, and data deletion.
  • No accounts, ads, subscriptions, analytics or cloud sync in this build.
